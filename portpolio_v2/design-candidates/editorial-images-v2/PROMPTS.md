## 04 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: a beautiful landscape 3:2 editorial cover graphic for case 04, a Korean product manager's portfolio about a reusable CMS.
Art direction: sophisticated Swiss typography meets contemporary digital product editorial, a striking designed composition, clean and confident, NOT an infographic template. Warm near-white backdrop #f3f1ec, near-black typography, precise dark cobalt #304db1 accents. Ultra crisp detail, restrained very subtle shadows.
Subject: a large beautifully typeset cropped word "SYSTEM" anchors the upper left. Below and to the right, a convincing elegantly art-directed collage of software interface fragments: one substantial off-white administrator window with a near-black sidebar, simple member rows and page-navigation controls, overlapped by two smaller clean content-editor panels and a small reservation calendar module. Emphasize a shared consistent modular interface visual language. All interface interiors are abstract graphic blocks, tidy rules and icons; no tiny fake text, names, personal data, metrics or lorem ipsum. Thin blue annotation strokes carefully link common navigation elements across the interface fragments. Small readable labels "COMMON" and "CUSTOM" only. Asymmetric layout, a little planar perspective for depth, plenty of intentional breathing room, strong hierarchy, sophisticated brochure cover quality. The interfaces are designed illustrative reconstructions, not screenshots of actual products.
Material: smooth digital surfaces, printed editorial texture almost imperceptible. Avoid physical paper stacks, paper sculptures, origami, clay blobs, glass, neon glow, floating bubbles, device mockups, gradients, stock-office imagery, people, logos, watermarks, long explanatory text. Result should feel like a highly designed product case-study cover, not a flowchart.
